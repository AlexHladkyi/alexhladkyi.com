Spiral

Task tracker for event organizers and coordinators

Designed the v1.0 mobile app for Spiral, a Basecamp-inspired task manager tailored for event organizers and on-site production teams. Serving as the sole UX/UI designer from discovery to interactive prototyping, I structured a multi-event dashboard that provides coordinators with a bird's-eye view of task progress and automated problem alerts. To streamline operations, I designed a decentralized communication system featuring contextual task-based chats and a cross-team request framework that empowers field staff to resolve issues directly without interrupting coordinators.

Keywords

Mobile Product Design, Task Management & Collaboration, 0→1 Project, Operations & Logistics, Project Management App, Communication Tools, Cross-Team Workflows

Attorneys on Demand

National court appearance service

Led the mobile product design for Attorneys on Demand, translating a complex, national web-based court appearance service into an intuitive universal app. Functioning as the sole designer from user flows to high-fidelity UI, I structured a tabbed, bottom-action navigation tailored for high-reachability on the go. The solution streamlines real-time case management by introducing prominent case update indicators and persistent data caching, ensuring critical legal notes and document uploads are safely stored until completion.

Keywords

Mobile Product Design, Legal Tech (LawTech), Web-to-Mobile Migration, Mobile-First UX/UI

Fannex

Fan engagement platform for live events

Designed the new mobile experience of Fannex, a live event fan engagement platform used by professional sports teams. I designed the new mobile app around a content-rich home feed and context-aware dynamic elements that automatically adjust based on game schedules, real-time scores, and physical stadium check-ins. The redesign focused on dual use cases: fast-paced, high-distraction stadium environments and casual at-home interaction. I also introduced an adaptable visual system for seamless white-label team branding.

Keywords

Mobile Product Design, Fan Engagement & Sports Tech, White-Label SaaS, Contextual / Proximity UX

Zyda Dashboard

Digital ordering and customer engagement

For Zyda I worked on UX/UI updates for the merchant configuration portal of a food order processing service. Working within the constraints of the Ant Design system, I tackled the complexity of real-time dual-language management by engineering a parallel English/Arabic layout for the menu setup module. This structural update eliminated toggle friction for administrators, creating a significantly less error-prone onboarding and menu-building workflow.

Keywords

B2B SaaS, Internationalization (i18n), Localization UX, Configuration & Dashboard Workflows
