LawLink

Functioning as the sole designer from initial discovery to developer handoff, I worked on a dual-sided experience featuring a free consumer portal and a premium directory for subscribing attorneys. The core service innovation centred on a ride-hailing-inspired emergency feature, allowing users to instantly broadcast their live audio and geolocation to nearby lawyers for immediate, real-time legal assistance.

My role

I was responsible for all design aspects of the project, except post-delivery design QA.

LawLink is a mobile-focused legal tech platform connecting clients with legal professionals.
