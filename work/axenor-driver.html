Axenor Driver

Truck fleet safety platforms typically use deduction-only scoring, so drivers start perfect and can only lose points. This breeds disengagement and pushback, especially when defensive driving is penalized.

Axenor needed a driver-facing mobile experience that turned real-time telematics into a safety score drivers could trust and actively improve, rewarding progress and recognition rather than just penalizing mistakes.

As the sole designer closely embedded with founders/eng, I helped shape and designed the MVP version of the app end-to-end.

My role

Discovery, core UX flows, visual direction and final mockups with dev handoff

Axenor.ai is a safety and risk reduction platform for trucking fleets that integrates telematics, in-cab signals, and operations data to drive coaching, incentives, and policy compliance.

The 0→1 app covered:

  • Surfacing safety scores and events that contribute to it to fleet drivers
  • Helping drivers track their daily and weekly safety goals
  • Showing estimated monetary end of the week bonuses
  • Light gamification elements like streaks, achievements and leaderboards

Android-specific adjustments

While a proper Android support was a low priority for v1, I've made a number of quick android-specific changes to make the app not feel too alien to the platform.
